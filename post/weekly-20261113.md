## Papers

### Bulletproofs are Optimal: Lower Bounds for Vector Commitments from Fiat-Shamir in Pairing-Free Groups
- Marian Dietz, Emanuele Giunta
- Proves lower bounds on opening proofs for constant-size vector commitments built via Fiat–Shamir in pairing-free generic groups.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2868>

### Revisiting Fiat-Shamir NIZKPs and Signatures from Seed-Friendly Sigma Protocols
- Andrea Flamini, Federico Pintore, Edoardo Signorini
- Shows that salted seed trees enable straight-line extraction for Fiat–Shamir proofs from seed-friendly Sigma protocols in the classical random-oracle model.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2858>

### ASCENT: Algebraic Structured Compilation of Exact Norms for Succinct Lattice Proofs
- Lizhen Zhang, Shang Gao, Bin Xiao
- Compiles exact Euclidean norm constraints for committed lattice witnesses into structured inner-product claims and relaxed shortness bounds.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2853>

### Delegatable Keyed-Verification Anonymous Credentials
- Omid Mirzamohammadi, Varesh Mishra, Jan Bobolz, Emad Heydari Beni, Aysajan Abidin, Dave Singelée, Bart Preneel
- Gives two pairing-free constructions that let holders delegate restricted versions of their keyed-verification anonymous credentials.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2860>

### BMRaccoon: Blind Multi-Signatures from Lattices
- Keerthi Aiswarya Varshini, Kanchan Bisht, Palkesh Jain, Maria Francis, R. Kabaleeshwaran
- Builds a two-round lattice-based blind multi-signature scheme that supports concurrent signing sessions and produces Raccoon-compatible signatures.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2870>

### Anamorphic NIZK: Definition, Construction and More
- Wenhui Kong, Gongxian Zeng, Zhengan Huang
- Constructs publicly verifiable zero-knowledge proofs that can carry covert messages recoverable with a secret key.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2866>

### Revisiting Witness-Succinct UC-lifting Compilers
- Davide Li Calsi, Luigi Russo, Dominique Schröder, Federico Valbusa
- Identifies a proof-rerandomization attack on a prior UC compiler and gives a new compiler that binds openings to their respective repetitions.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2453>

### SNARKs from LWE via Non-black-box Reductions
- Zhengzhong Jin, Mingqi Lu, Bo Peng
- Constructs SNARKs from LWE for certain languages with efficiently provable witness uniqueness, with an infinitely-often soundness guarantee.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2805>

### On Proximity Gaps for Expander Codes
- Kobi Gurkan, Andrija Novakovic, Ron D. Rothblum
- Establishes proximity gaps for low-degree expander codes, a property used to analyze soundness in proof systems.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2806>

### Polygero: Proximity Testing From Polynomial Challenges
- Matteo Frigo, abhi shelat
- Uses low-degree polynomial challenges for Reed–Solomon proximity testing in a Ligero-style IOP with Lean-checked soundness.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2481>

### Nowhere Man: Device Binding for Post-Quantum Anonymous Credentials
- Calvin Abou Haidar, Ryo Kikuchi, Octavio Perez-Kempner, Marcel Tiepelt
- Binds anonymous credentials to a device using ML-DSA signatures and zero-knowledge proofs at presentation.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2472>

### Post-Quantum Sublinear Lookup Arguments from Polynomial Preprocessing
- Adarsh Basumata, Chaya Ganesh, Varun Narayanan, Sikhar Patranabis, Surbhi Shaw, Nitin Singh
- Uses polynomial preprocessing and Reed–Muller proximity tests to obtain plausibly post-quantum lookups with sublinear online prover time.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2581>
