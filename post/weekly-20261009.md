## Papers

### The Lattice Isomorphism Problem with Hints
- Mélissa Rossi
- Studies LIP with partial secret information and refines Hawk key-recovery estimates, including an estimated security loss of about 11 bits for Hawk-1024.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2381>

### It Takes Two: Proofs of Work for Fiat–Shamir
- Benedikt Bünz, Jessica Chen, Ziyi Guan
- Shows that strong and non-amortizing PoW cannot coexist with a sufficiently cheap verifier, then combines two PoWs for grinding and diagonalization resistance.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2326>

### Ziren: Succinct Arguments for MIPS32 Execution
- Stephen Duan
- Combines a CPU-less chip architecture, jagged sumcheck, WHIR openings, and Lean-checked chip determinism to prove MIPS32 execution.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2330>

### Time-Space Tradeoffs For Probabilistic Proofs
- Alessandro Chiesa, Ziyi Guan, Omer Paneth, Nicholas Spooner
- Recommend: 🌟
- Establishes time-space tradeoffs for PCPs and IOPs with straightline knowledge soundness under sequential input access; random witness access enables an R1CS exception.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2406>

### Practical and Efficient MPC from FHE, without ZKPoKs
- Kelong Cong, Nigel P. Smart, Titouan Tanguy, Michael Walter
- Uses encrypted PRF functionality to remove ZK proofs of knowledge from a specific FHE-based MPC protocol and simplify its security proof.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2403>

### Truffle: Maliciously Secure Three-Party Shuffles with Applications to Parsing
- Nidhish Bhimrajka, Yashvanth Kondi, Daniel Noble, Bhavish Raj Gopal
- Uses distributed-verifier ZK proofs for shuffles secure against one active corruption, enabling efficient parsing of secret-shared strings.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2376>

### StOMR: Stateful Oblivious Message Retrieval
- Charles Gouert, Keewoo Lee, Dimitris Mouris, Yiannis Tselekounis, Jean-Luc Watson
- Recommend: 🌟
- Introduces lightweight private state for anonymous message retrieval, reporting sender signals approximately 178–650 times smaller than three prior systems.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2369>

### Faster Provable Lattice Sieving with Spherical Codes
- Divesh Aggarwal, Aditya Morolia, Noah Stephens-Davidowitz
- Combines fast nearest-neighbor methods with provably correct approximate-SVP sieving; runtime bounds depend on the spherical-code bounds or assumptions used.
- <https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2388>
