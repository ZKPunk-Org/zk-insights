### EVM Verification of WHIR over a 31-bit Field
- by Alex Kuzmin
- We implemented a Solidity verifier for standalone WHIR as a PCS over the 31-bit KoalaBear field. WHIR is a hash-based, transparent IOPP for constrained Reed-Solomon codes that is plausibly post-quantum sound.
- <https://pse.dev/blog/evm-verification-of-whir-31bit>
